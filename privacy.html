Privacy Policy

TGS Studios (Joseph Marini) · Apps: Rock Rank · Effective November 10, 2026

The short version

Rock Rank does not collect, store or share your personal information. There are no user accounts, no usernames or passwords, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your settings (such as the screen theme), your game results, your own top-artist lists and any artists you suggest are saved only on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

Premium subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not see or store your name, payment details or Apple or Google account. The app only asks the store whether Premium is active on your account.

Links to other services

Some pages may link to other websites. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

Rock Rank does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
