Rock Rank Support

Questions, a missing artist, or a problem with Premium? Email tgstgs83@gmail.com and we’ll get back to you.

Restore Premium on a new phone

Open Rock Rank, go to Your Account (or Why Premium) and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple or Google account you bought Premium with.

Cancel a subscription

iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Rock Rank. Android: Google Play → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

How the rankings work

In the app, open How We Score Them for the seven parts of every Rank Score.
